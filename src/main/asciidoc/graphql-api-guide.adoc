[[schema]]
== Schema

The schema is `src/main/resources/graphql/schema.graphqls`; each field says who may call it. The endpoint is
`POST /api/graphql`. It answers without a token too, with what visitors may see.

[[errors]]
== Errors

Errors carry `extensions.classification`: `UNAUTHORIZED`, `FORBIDDEN`, `BAD_REQUEST`, `NOT_FOUND`, `CONFLICT` (someone
else saved first, or a stale version) or `SPEXREGISTER_UNAVAILABLE`. Messages are in the request's language
(`Accept-Language`, Swedish by default).

[[roles]]
== Roles and visibility

Client roles on `fgv-spexipedia`: `USER` (every approved user), `AUTHOR`, `EDITOR`, `ADMIN`; and for services `SYNC`
(the change feed) and `REVIVAL` (creating revivals). Base data is public, published or not. Pages are shown to
visitors when published; members' articles to `USER`.

[[people]]
== People

People come from Spexregister when a page is asked for, never from Spexipedia's database. Visitors get published
spexare's nicknames only; `USER` also their names. If Spexregister cannot be reached, `Spex.people.available` is false
and the rest of the page is answered.

[[photos]]
== Photos and links

Photos of people are Spexgram items: `PhotoSet` holds what the viewer may see and, for logged-in users without access to
Spexgram, how many they may not. Links to Spexgram, Spexify, Spexflix and Spexarkivet are shown only to users with access
to that application, except Spexgram's public albums.

[[feed]]
== The change feed

`baseDataChanges(after, first)` lists changes to spex categories, spex, task categories, tasks, characters and numbers in
order, each with the record as it was right after the change. Apply them in order and keep `cursor`; pass it as `after`
next time. `MERGE` means: move references from `id` to `mergedIntoId`. The caller needs `SYNC`; the cursor it reads up
to is shown to admins.

After a change, Spexipedia calls each consumer's notify address with its service token (`NOTIFY` on the consumer's
client); the call carries no data.
